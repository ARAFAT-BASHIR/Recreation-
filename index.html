ABOUT US

A Place to Relax & Enjoy

Kiteezi Recreational Center is a destination for relaxation, recreation, great food and memorable experiences.

Whether you want to take a swim, enjoy sports, have a meal with friends and family, or simply relax in a comfortable environment, we have something for you.

We are located in Kiteezi, opposite Shell Kiteezi, Uganda.
